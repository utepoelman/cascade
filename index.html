Rules of Cascade

🎯 Objective:
  • Solo Challenge: Place ALL 24 red marbles onto the board gates with 0 marbles reaching the bottom tray!
  • Multiplayer Match: Everyone starts with the same number of marbles (12 by default). Be the FIRST player to empty your hand!

Turn Rules:

  • The gates start in random positions and the first player is chosen at random.
  • On your turn you must drop at least 1 marble and may drop up to 3 (one at a time). You can end your turn after any drop.
  • If any marble falls all the way through to the bottom tray, you must take ALL marbles that land there and your turn ends immediately.

Flip-Flop Gate Rules:

  • Empty Gate:
    • Entering on BLOCKED side: Settles into the upper cup and stays caught.
    • Entering on OPEN side: Passes straight through and FLIPS the gate!
  • Occupied Gate & Spillover Gap:
    • When a marble arrives at a gate already holding a marble, it CANNOT enter the cup.
    • It slides through the prominent cutout gap in the wall into the adjacent path!
    • Going down the adjacent path FLIPS the gate and RELEASING the trapped marble on that side!
  • Bottom Collection Tray: Any marble reaching the bottom rests in the tray for 1 second before returning to your hand, and your turn ends!
